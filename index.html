How to play

  • Tap a road or junction to see what you can build there, and what it costs.
  • Spend the budget on lanes, turn lanes, roundabouts, new roads, or buses. Undo is free.
  • Watch the meter: it shows how much rush-hour delay your plan removes.
  • Fixing one jam moves it. Follow the busiest roads downstream before you submit.
  • Consultant names the worst road and the best fix, for a fee from your budget.
